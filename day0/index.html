Model CI pass rate CI coverage Recipe image Recipe GPUs AMD PRs
CI pass rate is the fraction of this model's CI jobs whose newest current result passes. A model CI job runs only this model's tests. A job with no finished run in the window has no result and is left out of the rate; a result more than 3 days older than the newest nightly is stale and does not pass. Disaggregation (DI) nightly configs are left out of this rate.
CI coverage tracks every recipe-required MI30x / MI35x × FP4 / FP8 cell. FP4 includes MXFP4; FP8 includes MXFP8. Model and shared jobs both count as coverage; only model CI jobs decide pass rate.
Recipe GPUs are the AMD GPUs listed in the cookbook recipe. They are documentation claims, not the machines CI ran on; CI runner families appear in the CI job details.
Recipe image is the Docker image the recipe tells AMD users to run: a tagged release, a dated nightly, or a model-specific build. Docker Hub is checked for the exact tag.
AMD PRs name the model and have an AMD title tag, known AMD author, or AMD-only vendor label. The PR-series badge follows upstream naming; it is not a timing metric.
AMD checkpoints are huggingface.co/amd repos for the model, excluding client (Ryzen AI, ONNX, GGUF) and NVIDIA-format builds.
Cause and Fix PRs under a failing model come from the CI monitor's daily report for the failing run, as on the Nightly CI page. A cause is the bot's hypothesis, not a verified bisect.
Warnings are collapsed under Signals; open it to inspect and jump to affected models.
